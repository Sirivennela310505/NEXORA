Industry Challenge

The Broken Student Learning Funnel

Why traditional online courses fail 90% of students before job readiness

1. Static, One-Size-Fits-All Playlists

Beginners and career switchers are forced through the same 100-hour video courses, causing boredom on known topics and severe dropouts on difficult concepts.

2. Undetected Prerequisite Gaps

Students attempt complex topics (e.g. Dynamic Programming, Rotational Dynamics) without mastering required foundational mechanics, resulting in repeated failures.

3. Analysis Paralysis

With thousands of disorganized YouTube videos and tutorials, students don't know what single high-yield action to invest their time in today.

4. The Resume & ATS Disconnect

Learners finish tutorials but struggle to translate what they built into ATS-compliant bullet points and measurable recruiter-ready impact metrics.
